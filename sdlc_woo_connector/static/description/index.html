FAQs

Questions buyers ask us most.

Don't see yours? Email us at support@sdlccorp.com and we'll get back same business day.

Which Odoo versions are supported?

Odoo 16, 17, and 18 — both Community and Enterprise editions. The connector is tested against fresh installs and common module combinations before each release.

Which WooCommerce versions does it work with?

WooCommerce 5.x and above, on WordPress 5.6+. The integration uses the standard WooCommerce REST API, so no custom WordPress plugins or theme modifications are required on your store.

Is the sync real-time, or scheduled?

Both. Orders and inventory sync via WooCommerce webhooks (near-instant). A cron-based fallback runs on a schedule you control, so any record missed by a webhook is reconciled automatically. You can also trigger a manual full sync from the dashboard.

Can I connect multiple WooCommerce stores to one Odoo?

Yes. Add as many stores as you need. Each one keeps its own catalog, customers, orders, and currency settings inside Odoo, while sharing your single Odoo backend for fulfillment, accounting, and reporting.

What happens if a sync fails?

Failures are logged in the Sync Reports view with full error context — the request payload, the response, and a stack trace if applicable. Most can be re-triggered with a single click. For repeated failures, you can configure email or Slack alerts so your team is notified within minutes.

Is implementation help included?

Yes. Every purchase includes 10 hours of expert-led implementation (valued at US $500) — covering installation, configuration, field mapping, sync rules, and a guided first end-to-end test against your live store.

What about ongoing support?

90 days of email support is included with every license — bug fixes, configuration questions, and sync diagnostics. Extended support and SLA-backed plans are available; reach out to sales@sdlccorp.com for pricing.

What's your refund policy?

We follow the standard Odoo Apps Store policy: refunds are available within 30 days of purchase if the connector cannot be made to work in your environment. Reach out to support and we'll either resolve the issue or process the refund — whichever you prefer.
